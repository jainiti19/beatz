R2 points: instrumental = 2 · bass only = 3 · drums only = 5.
R3: two lines minimum, table sings together. Two passes allowed, then a point comes off.
R4: spoken answers count. Be generous — the aim is keeping everyone in, not accuracy.
Runaway table: freeze their scoring for two songs. Say it laughing — it's a handicap, not a punishment.
Write these down the same night — this is the point of the whole run:
1. Did the quietest person sing in Round 3?  Who?
2. Did anyone sing in Round 5 who wasn't nominated?  How many?
3. Where did the energy dip?  What time, and what was happening?

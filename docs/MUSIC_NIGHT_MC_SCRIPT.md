# Music Night — MC Script & Cue Sheet

## Companion to `MUSIC_NIGHT_FORMAT.md`
## Hinglish, for a female MC. ~3 hours, 5–6 tables.

**Print this. Keep it on the laptop table with the scoresheet.**

Golden rule for the whole night: **the MC's job is energy and enforcement, not
performance.** Do not sing until Round 5, and even then only if a table is short.

---

## Before anyone arrives

- [ ] Laptop + PA tested at volume (see audio checklist in requirements doc)
- [ ] Lyrics page open on the TV/projector, dark background, tested from the far table
- [ ] Playlist loaded, Ninjago tracks and duplicate *Tum Hi Ho* removed
- [ ] Table tent cards out — one song name per table
- [ ] Nomination cards + pens on every table (face down, don't explain yet)
- [ ] Scoresheet printed, pen that works
- [ ] Karan briefed: **anchor and host, not headliner.** Opens Round 5, closes it,
      rescues gaps, off the mic in between.

---

## 0:00 — Opening (2 minutes, no longer)

> "Achha sab log, sun lo! Welcome to Bollywood Night!"
>
> "Aaj raat do cheezein honi hain. Pehla — hum khelenge. Doosra — hum gaayenge."
>
> "But — koi tension nahi. **Shuruaat mein koi gaana nahi hai.** Bas shouting.
> Aur shouting mein toh hum sab expert hain, haan?"
>
> *[laugh, wait for it]*
>
> "Rule number one — **aapki table hi aapki team hai.** Jahan baithe ho, wahi
> aapka group. Koi idhar-udhar nahi. Table ka naam dekh lo — sabke saamne card
> rakha hai."
>
> "Points milenge. Scoreboard main rakhungi. Aur end mein ek winning table hoga."
>
> "Chalo — Round One. Naam hai **Do Second**."

**Why it's short:** every extra minute of explaining is a minute the room isn't
loud. Get to Round 1 fast.

---

## 0:02 — Round 1: "Do Second" / Two Seconds (20 min)

**Rule:** two seconds of a song. First table to shout the correct name gets the point.

> "Main gaane ke sirf **do second** bajaungi. Jo table pehle naam chillayegi —
> point unka. Bas naam, poora gaana nahi!"
>
> "Ready? Pehla gaana… "

**Cue sheet — full mix, first 2 seconds. 1 point each.**
Start with the unmistakable ones. Order matters — the first three must be easy.

| # | Song | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chaiyya Chaiyya | Opens on the train rhythm — instant |
| 2 | Kala Chashma | Everyone gets this |
| 3 | London Thumakda | Everyone gets this |
| 4 | Badtameez Dil | |
| 5 | Ghungroo | |
| 6 | Munni Badnaam | |
| 7 | Tujhe Dekha Toh | Classic — older crowd will pounce |
| 8 | Kal Ho Naa Ho | |
| 9 | Tum Hi Ho | |
| 10 | Channa Mereya | |
| 11 | Ghagra | |

**If a table is running away with it:** *"Arre, doosri tables ko bhi mauka do!
Ab is table ka jawab count nahi hoga agle do gaano mein."* Say it laughing — it's
a handicap, not a punishment.

**If the room is slow to start:** play four seconds instead of two for the first
three songs. Do not announce the change.

## 0:22 — Round 2: "Kaun Baja Raha Hai" / Name That Stem (20 min)

**Rule:** same game, but instruments only. Harder = more points.

> "Ab thoda mushkil. Ab main gaana bajaungi — **lekin ek instrument nikaal doongi.
> Ya sirf ek instrument bajaungi.**"
>
> "Instrumental — do point. Sirf bass — teen point. Aur sirf dhol, sirf drums —
> **paanch point**, kyunki woh impossible hai!"

**Cue sheet:**

| Points | Stem | Songs |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Instrumental (vocals removed) | Senorita (ZNMD), Ilahi, Kabira, Agar Tum Saath Ho |
| 3 | Bass only | Kala Chashma, Ghagra, London Thumakda |
| 5 | Drums only | Hudd Hudd Dabangg, Munni Badnaam, Badtameez Dil |

Save a drums-only for last. Nobody will get it and the whole room will laugh —
that's the point, and it's the right note to end a shouting round on.

---

## 0:42 — Round 3: Antakshari, table vs table (30 min)

**This is where singing starts. It starts as a group. Say so explicitly.**

> "Ab asli cheez. **Antakshari!**"
>
> "Rules sab jaante hain — jo aakhri akshar aaye, ussi se agla gaana. **Do line
> minimum.**"
>
> "Aur sun lo — ye **table gaayegi, akela banda nahi.** Poori table. Jo bhi aata
> ho, saath mein gao. Suron ki koi zaroorat nahi — bas volume chahiye!"
>
> "Do baar pass kar sakte ho. Uske baad point cut."

**No backing track. Fully a cappella, from memory.** Beatz stays quiet this round.

**Run it:** point at a table, they sing, you call the last letter loudly and point
at the next table. Keep it moving — 20 seconds per table maximum.

**Watch the quietest person at each table.** If they're mouthing along, the format
is working. That is the single most important observation of the whole evening.

**If a table freezes:** *"Chalo, main pehli line deti hoon — phir aap."* Give them
the opening line and hand it back. Never let a table sit in silence more than five
seconds; the fear is contagious.

---

## 1:12 — Round 4: "Line Poori Karo" / Finish the Line (15 min)

**Rule:** a lyric appears on the screen with a gap. The table completes it.
**Spoken answers count.** Be generous — the point is keeping everyone in.

> "Screen pe line aayegi — beech se ek tukda gayab. Table ko poori karni hai."
>
> "Gaa ke bolo, ya bas bol do — dono chalega. Point milega."

**Preparing the cues:** don't write these by hand. The app already has Whisper
timed lyrics for every processed song (`web/stems/<song>/lyrics_timed.json`).
Pick 8–10 songs, choose a well-known line from the existing lyrics data, and blank
the second half. Use songs from Round 1 — the room already has them in its head.

### ⚠️ The critical moment — nominations (do this mid-round, not at the end)

Roughly ten minutes into Round 4, while the room is still playing:

> "Rukho, ek kaam. Table pe jo card rakha hai — palto."
>
> "Har table ko **ek naam aur ek gaana** likhna hai. Ek banda jo aapki table ki
> taraf se gaayega. Aur kaunsa gaana."
>
> "Table decide karegi — akela banda volunteer nahi karega. **Ye table ka faisla
> hai.** Do minute. Chalo."

*[Collect all cards. Do not read them out.]*

**Why mid-round and not at the end:** the room is warm and distracted, so it feels
like part of the game rather than a summons. Ask at the end and it becomes a
spotlight, which is the thing this entire design exists to avoid.

**Why the table decides:** going up stops being an act of individual bravery and
becomes a team decision. Completely different ask.

**If a table refuses to nominate:** *"Theek hai — main choose kar loongi."* Then
pick the person who was loudest in Antakshari. They've already sung; they just
don't know they're ready.

---

## 1:27 — Scores, then the mic opens

Announce scores. Announce the winner. **Close the competition properly** — it must
be clearly over, so the second half isn't a contest.

> "Aur aaj raat ki winning table hai — **[table]**! Taaliyan!"
>
> "Bas, competition khatam. Ab points nahi. **Ab sirf gaana.**"
>
> "Aur mere paas aapke cards hain. Toh chalo — [Karan] shuru karega, phir line
> lagi hai."

---

## 1:30 — Round 5: Open Mic (60–75 min)

**Karan opens.** One song, done well, sets the standard and shows the format works.
Then straight into the nominated queue — no gap, no asking.

**Running order:** Karan → nominated singers in the order the cards came in →
anyone else who volunteers (they will, once three people have gone) → Karan closes.

**Beatz:** karaoke preset (vocals removed), lyrics projected, song pre-loaded before
the previous singer finishes. **Never a silent gap between singers** — that is when
the energy dies and people reach for phones.

**MC job here:** introduce each singer by name and table, clap first and loudest,
get off the mic. Do not comment on the singing.

### Recovery lines

| Problem | Say this |
|---|---|
| Nominated singer refuses | *"Koi baat nahi — table se koi bhi. Saath mein gao!"* Turn a solo into a duet rather than losing the slot. |
| Room going quiet | *"Chorus sab log! Ye gaana sab ko aata hai!"* Pull the room in rather than pushing the singer harder. |
| Someone hogging the mic | *"Ek aur? Nahi nahi, line lagi hai bhai!"* Laughing. Then next card, immediately. |
| Tech fails / song won't load | *"Antakshari! Jab tak ye theek ho, ek round!"* Antakshari needs no tech — it is the universal fallback all night. |
| Nobody wants to follow Karan | Read the first card and call them **by name from their table.** Named + team-mandated is very hard to refuse. |

---

## 2:45 — Round 6: Free for all

Vocals back in. Dance numbers. Ghagra, Kala Chashma, London Thumakda, Hudd Hudd
Dabangg, Munni Badnaam. No structure, no MC. Let it become a party.

---

## After — the three things to write down

Do this the same night, before you forget. This is the whole point of running it.

1. **Did the quietest person sing in Round 3?** Yes / no. Who.
2. **Did anyone sing in Round 5 who wasn't nominated?** How many.
3. **Where did energy dip?** Exact time and what was happening.

Those three answers decide whether this format is a product or just a nice evening.
